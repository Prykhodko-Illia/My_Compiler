   What the AI did (Task 1 - unused variables):
   Created the practice-9 branch. New file src/optimizer.py: UseCounter, one visitor over the
   whole tree that counts the reads of every DeclNode keyed by id(decl), through the checker's

   read of its own declaration, and of the declaration an assignment writes); Pruner, which
   rebuilds every statement list without the dead declarations and the assignments to them, at
   the top level, in if / while blocks, in function, member function and lambda bodies; and
   remove_unused, the loop that stops when a round removes nothing. A lambda is not removed: its
   captures count as reads and its body is walked. compiler.py: the flags may stand anywhere,
   --remove-unused runs the pass after check() and before codegen, --ast-checked prints the tree
   after it, an unknown flag prints the usage. Checked both warm-ups (the second one's tree line

   printing call, a self-feeding k, an emptied if block, and one with structs, a member function
   and a lambda. Ran all 240 earlier tests without the flag and, through a sed-edited copy of
   run_tests.sh, with it: 240 passing both ways. Committed as "Task 1: ...".
