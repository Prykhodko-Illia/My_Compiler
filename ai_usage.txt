   What the AI did:
   Read the PDF and wrote no code. Found that my practice-6 branch had been created from a stale
   local main (the Practice 4 merge) and was missing all of Practice 5, which was only on
   origin/main, and gave me the fast-forward command. Summarised the four tasks and explained the
   biggest changes to the language: types are no longer three fixed words but user-defined structs,
   so the semantic pass needs a struct table and gives field indices to the generator; mut becomes
   deep (every link of a chain); a program becomes several functions, so exit has two meanings, a
   body needs its own scope stack that cannot see top-level variables, signatures must be collected

   parser needs two tokens of look-ahead for "Point p{...}" against "p := ...". Listed open
